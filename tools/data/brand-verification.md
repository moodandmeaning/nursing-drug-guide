# Israeli brand-name verification log

Source: wikitrufot.org.il (mirror of the Ministry of Health drug registry, pages updated 2025-2026).
It confirms a brand when found, but is incomplete - "not found" does NOT prove a brand is absent in Israel.
The MoH registry itself (israeldrugs.health.gov.il) blocks automated lookups.

## Corrected after checking
- Pravastatin: was Pravachol/Pravalip -> Prava Teva, Pravastatin Teva
- Bezafibrate: was Bezalip Retard -> Norlip, Bezafibrate Medomie
- Amiloride: was Moduretic/Midamor -> Kaluril
- Bivalirudin: was Angiox -> Bivalirudin Medi-Market
- Lercanidipine: removed Zanidip (not an Israeli product)
- Betahistine: Betaserc -> Betahistine Teva
- Tranexamic acid: Hebrew spelling הקסאקפרון
